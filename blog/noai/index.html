AI? Nah.

2026-02-06

There are sufficient non-selfish reasons to reject AI. I’m here to talk about my perspective as a CS student. I did use AI for a lot of silly little projects and dumb questions where I don’t actually care about the answer (especially in the evening when I’m tired or drunk). While I never paid for any service, the agents I did use, worked “well enough”, but never great. My confidence in the generated code was always quite low. Every so often I would read a nice piece on why AI is unethical and then avoid it for a few weeks, only open Gemini one evening to:

Are there cables faster than fiber optic that actually approache the speed of light?

— actual prompt, by me; the answer is obviously yes, but there are also Hollow-core Fibers

After feeling depressed by the general state of the world from reading Garfield’s article today, I made the realization:

Every time I used AI, I should have either:

  • done something actually entertaining, I still have great movies to watch
  • did proper research and find the interesting rabbit holes
  • implemented it myself

That last point is very important. I’m not at a stage where I know everything, and until I’m 30-40 my mind will have a much easier time learning new stuff. So I should implement my ideas myself for my own sake. I get to know about the interesting details of the stuff I interact with. I get to properly use existing libraries, written by experts on the topic. An AI would just follow whatever my misguided assumptions on how the something should work is.

Furthermore: whatever little “prompt engineering” I learn from using AI will be almost certainly useless by the time I could put it to good use. Given the amount of money in the market, I don’t believe LLMs will stay affordable for consumers, like me. Every technology has a ceiling, and teams like DeepSeek just about reached it. The only thing that can change now is the user interface. Which, by the way, will get worse, like the internet as a whole has shown.

So if I end up writing stupid stuff with AI again: Please hold me accountable to this post, thanks.
