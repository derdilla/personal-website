If you want to spend less time scrolling and more time reading something a little more long form may I suggest _books_? For some of them you don't even need to go visit a bookshop or your local library. I personally liked the ones below, though I probably immediately forgot the best ones after reading them in a single sitting.


## Harry Potter and the methods of rationality

Surprisingly fun book about a smart Harry Potter trying to use science do understand how the magical world works. This is not a collection of hypothetical experiments in a magical world, but a real story with a complex plot, some own world building and character development. This is what really introduced me to the whole ratfic genre.

- Canonically at [https://hpmor.com/](https://hpmor.com/)
- A really great audiobook version at [https://www.hpmorpodcast.com/](https://www.hpmorpodcast.com/)


## Unsong

What if the moon landing made biblical god's magic run loose in the world? Includes lots of fun plays on words and an really enlightening analysis of Don McLean's American Pie.

Assumes a little familiarity with christian/jewish religion for some of the jokes to land. Most of the story will work without though. It is really well written and if you can find the time I encourage you to give it a read/listen. In the meantime: don't boil a goat in its mother's milk.

- Canonically at [https://unsongbook.com/](https://unsongbook.com/)
- Podcast/Audiobook version at [https://unsong.libsyn.com/](https://unsong.libsyn.com/)
